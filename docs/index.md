# Step-by-Step Math Notes

Short, clear notes on common school and college math topics: quadratic equations, percentages, systems of linear equations, the chain rule and integration by parts.

## How to use these notes

1. Read the worked example for the topic.
2. Try the practice problem yourself.
3. Check each step of your answer.

If you want every step of your own problem explained, you can use [MathGPT, a free AI math solver](https://mathssolvergpt.com/en) that shows step-by-step solutions for algebra, geometry and calculus.

## Quadratic equations

For \(ax^2 + bx + c = 0\), the roots are \(x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}\). Example: \(x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0\) gives \(x = 2\) or \(x = 3\).

## Percentages

To find 15% of 80, multiply 0.15 by 80 to get 12.

## Chain rule

If \(y = f(g(x))\), then \(y' = f'(g(x)) \cdot g'(x)\). Example: the derivative of \((3x+1)^5\) is \(15(3x+1)^4\).
