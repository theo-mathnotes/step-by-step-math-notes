# Percentage problems

A percentage is a fraction of 100. "25% of 80" means \(0.25 \times 80\). In multi-step money problems, apply each percentage to the correct base. A discount is taken from the original price; sales tax is usually taken from the price after the discount, not from the original tag.

## Worked example

A jacket costs \$80. The store takes 25% off, and then 8% sales tax is added to the discounted price. What is the final amount paid?

After a 25% discount, 75% of the price remains:

\[
0.75 \times 80 = 60.
\]

Tax is 8% of that discounted price:

\[
0.08 \times 60 = 4.80.
\]

The total is \(60 + 4.80 = \$64.80\).

You can also combine the factors: \(0.75 \times 1.08 = 0.81\), and \(80 \times 0.81 = 64.80\).

## Checks and common mistakes

- Tax should be computed on \$60, not on \$80. Adding 8% of the original price would incorrectly give \(60 + 6.40 = 66.40\).
- Taking 25% off and then 8% off would be a second discount, not a tax.
- The combined factor 0.81 is a useful mental check: 81% of 80 is 64.80.

## A short follow-up

A score of 42 out of 50 is \(42/50 = 0.84\), or 84%. If the passing mark is 80%, the score is 4 percentage points above the cutoff. To reach 90%, you would have needed \(0.90 \times 50 = 45\) correct answers, so three more than 42.
